The paperwork.
In plain language.

Nine short documents, written to be read by a student, a parent and a teacher rather than a lawyer. Each one says what happens, what does not happen, and what this project cannot promise.

Last updated 27 October 2026

01 / Read these first

  • Terms of use — what you may do here, and the limits of a school project.
  • Privacy policy — what is stored, where, and how to get or delete it.
  • Acceptable use & academic integrity — the one rule that matters: this helps you learn, it does not do your work.

02 / Data and devices

  • Cookies & local storage — the six things saved in your browser, and how to clear them.
  • Sub-processors & transfers — every outside service your data can reach.
  • Security & trust — the protections in place and how to report a problem.

03 / Content and answers

  • AI answer disclaimer — answers can be wrong; check the citation.
  • Copyright & takedown — what is ours, what is not, and how to ask us to remove something.
  • Accessibility statement — the standard aimed for and the gaps that remain.

04 / Reading the dates

Each document carries the date it last changed. A material change to what is collected, or to who receives it, is noted in the release notes and, where an account is involved, shown in the app after sign-in. Documents are versioned with the app: this set belongs to release 2.46.0. No document on this site is legal advice, and none of them is a substitute for your school's own rules.

05 / Who to ask

Questions, corrections and objections go to the project operator — the student, the teacher in charge, or the school office of Vignan Vidyanikethan English Medium High School. Security problems should go through the private route described in Security instead of a public issue. Machine-readable contact details are at /.well-known/security.txt.
