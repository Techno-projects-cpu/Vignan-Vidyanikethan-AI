Sub-processors
& transfers.

Every outside service this app can talk to, what it is told, and the switch that turns it off. If a service is not on this page, the app does not call it.

Last updated 27 October 2026

01 / How to read this page

A sub-processor is an outside service that may receive data while the app does its job. Some of them are only used if you turn a feature on or type a key in Settings; those are marked optional. The list is short on purpose, and the browser's Content Security Policy only permits these addresses, so a missing entry would be a bug rather than a hidden route.

02 / Hosting and storage

Render (hosting) serves the pages and runs the small Node server; it sees ordinary request metadata such as IP address and user agent. Supabase (optional account storage) holds account records — username, age band, progress, friends, chat — when an operator configures an accounts backend; without it the app keeps accounts in a local JSON store on the server. Neither is used for advertising, and neither receives your imported books.

03 / AI providers

Optional, and only the one you choose. Requests go from your browser straight to the provider you selected (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral, Cohere, DeepSeek, Together, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI, or Arena), or through this site's proxy when you use the school key order. The provider receives the question, the context the app assembled for it, and your key if you supplied one. It does not receive your chat history beyond that request, your books, or your account record. Each provider is bound by its own terms and retention policy.

04 / Search and reference

Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo receive your search query when the free search fallback is used — the words you asked, not your identity. The fallback can be switched off in Settings; when it is off, answers rely on the model and on pages you already have open. Hugging Face is contacted once, on your own device, to download the model files for the keyless on-device engine; those files are cached and the request carries no question.

05 / Your own machine

If you run a local model (Ollama on port 11434 or LM Studio on port 1234), the app talks to your own computer and nothing leaves it. That route is listed in the policy only because a browser needs permission to reach it at all.

06 / What none of them get

No advertising identifiers, no behavioural profile, no cross-site tracking, no selling or sharing of data, and no upload of your textbooks or notes by the project itself. There is no analytics vendor on this list because there is no analytics vendor. Links the app tokenises for a citation are attributed to the page they came from, not to a tracker.

07 / Changes

Adding a sub-processor is a release-level change: it must be added here and to the Content Security Policy in the same commit, and the release notes say so. If a service is removed, it is struck from this page in the next release rather than left as history. This document belongs to release 2.45.1.
