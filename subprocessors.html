Sub-processors
& transfers.

Outside services and configurable destinations: what they may receive and how to control their use. The exact services depend on this deployment and your settings.

Last updated 27 October 2026

01 / How to read this page

A sub-processor is an outside service that may receive data while the app does its job. Some of them are only used if you turn a feature on or type a key in Settings; those are marked optional. The browser's Content Security Policy limits browser connections, but does not constrain the server's outbound calls. Configured server providers, search services and public websites you ask the server to read can also receive requests.

02 / Hosting and storage

Render (hosting) serves the pages and runs the small Node server; it sees ordinary request metadata such as IP address and user agent. Supabase (optional account storage) holds account records — username, age band, progress, friends, chat — when an operator configures an accounts backend; without it the app keeps accounts in a local JSON store on the server. Neither is used for advertising, and neither receives your imported books.

03 / AI providers

Hosted AI may be selected automatically on new installations when the site reports it is configured, or when Nezaad is activated. Check Settings or choose Offline mode before sending sensitive material. Requests go from your browser straight to the provider you selected (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Mistral, Cohere, DeepSeek, Together, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI, or Arena), or through this site's proxy when you use the school key order or hosted defaults. The server may use Bytez (api.bytez.com), OpenRouter and other configured providers, and may retry across them. These routing services can pass the request to the model provider they use. The provider receives the question, the context the app assembled for it, and your key if you supplied one. The request can include conversation context and relevant excerpts from your books, not just the latest question. Full library/account synchronisation is not part of the AI route. Each provider is bound by its own terms and retention policy.

04 / Search and reference

Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo receive your search query when the free search fallback is used — the words you asked, not your identity. The fallback can be switched off in Settings; when it is off, answers rely on the model and on pages you already have open. Hugging Face, its model-file CDNs and GitHub raw file hosting may be contacted to download on-device model files; those files are cached but can be downloaded again after eviction or a model change. Model downloads do not carry your question. Configured search providers and up to two top-result websites may receive server requests. A public URL you explicitly ask the app to read also contacts its destination even when search fallback is off.

05 / Your own machine

If you run a local model (Ollama on port 11434 or LM Studio on port 1234), the app talks to the endpoint you configured. A localhost endpoint runs on your own computer; a remote endpoint sends prompts to that machine. The operator of that endpoint controls its logging and retention. That route is listed in the policy only because a browser needs permission to reach it at all.

06 / What none of them get

No advertising identifiers, no behavioural profile, no cross-site tracking, no selling or sharing of data, and no routine whole-library upload. Relevant textbook or note excerpts can be included in connected-AI requests, and social messages are stored on the configured backend. There is no analytics vendor on this list because there is no analytics vendor. Links the app tokenises for a citation are attributed to the page they came from, not to a tracker.

07 / Changes

Adding a sub-processor is a release-level change: it must be documented here; browser-connected destinations must also be permitted by the Content Security Policy, and the release notes say so. If a service is removed, it is struck from this page in the next release rather than left as history. This document belongs to release 2.46.2.
