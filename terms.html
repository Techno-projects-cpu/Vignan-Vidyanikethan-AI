A few ground rules for using this student-built study project responsibly.

Last updated 28 October 2026

01 / What this is

Vignan Vidyanikethan AI is an educational demonstration made by a student of Vignan Vidyanikethan English Medium High School for the INSPIRE-MANAK science fair. It brings textbook search and existing AI tools together; it is not a newly trained model. You may use it as a guest or choose to create an account where available.

02 / Study with judgement

Answers, translations, citations, generated quizzes, grading, model papers and web results can be incomplete, incorrect or out of date. Model papers may reuse uploaded questions, do not predict an exam and have no inferred answer key. Open the cited pages and check important information with a teacher or reliable source. The app is a study aid, not a substitute for professional advice, official assessment or your school's rules. Leaderboard scores are self-reported by browsers and are not verified exam results.

03 / Your account and material

Keep passwords and API keys private, use a non-identifying username, and do not access another person's account or interfere with the service. You are responsible for material you upload or share. Only upload textbooks and other content you have permission to use, and follow any copyright or licence restrictions. Do not upload sensitive personal information.

If you are under 13, get a parent or guardian's permission before registering and ask an adult to help with connected services. Schools and operators must make their own arrangements for any legally required consent; the app's checkbox is not independently verified.

04 / Connected tools and sharing

AI providers, web search, model downloads and outside links have separate terms and may have usage limits. The labelled web-search switch is enabled for new installations and can be switched off in the chat box or at the top of Settings. Reading a public link is a separate request to that site. You are responsible for any keys you provide. A video, poster, caption or invite that you share can reveal its contents to others; get permission before sharing information about anyone else. Zero Vast is an external site, not part of this study app.

05 / Availability and changes

This is a free project provided as available. Features may change, be interrupted or be removed, and local browser data may be cleared by the device or browser. Keep your own copies of anything important. Nothing here takes away rights you may have under applicable law.

06 / Privacy and review

Read the privacy policy for details about local storage, accounts, external AI, search and sharing. If you have questions about this deployment, ask the school or project operator. No dedicated contact channel has been supplied. The operator should review these terms and add appropriate contact information before public use.
