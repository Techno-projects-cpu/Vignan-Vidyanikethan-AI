Fourteen plates, framed instead of covered

Painted just as carefully and used differently: their colours are too evenly spread to put text over, so they hang as pictures with nothing written across them. Five of them are the accents worn inside the seven night rooms. The numbers after each caption are the measured share of dominant, secondary and accent — the reason each one hangs here and not behind a panel.

Set any of them in the app under Settings, then Style. Minimal and Legacy are both still there — the original look was never taken away.

03

“It will not answer from nothing. If your page does not cover it, it says so, and only then offers a search, clearly labelled as outside your books.”

Most school AI projects are a wrapper around a chat model: ask anything, get a confident paragraph, check nothing. This one refuses when the evidence is thin.

It is not a newly trained model either. It is an orchestrator: retrieval over your own pages, a citation that has to name a page, a rule that stops the answer when the page is not there, and a local model option for when the cloud is not available.

04

Daylight,
under one sheet of glass.

Glass Morphism is the default, and it is the only theme with no photograph in it. Pink, sand, turquoise and peach are painted by the browser itself; white glass sits on top; every panel is a pane of that glass, lit along its top-left edge and shaded along its bottom-right, the way a real slab catches a room.

  • Four fields, one sheet. The pane is white at 34%, so the classroom is legible through a panel; the inks are deep enough to clear 7:1 over the darkest pixel of the painting.
  • It refracts, where the browser can. On Chromium the backdrop bends at the rim, so the fields slide under the edge of a panel. Everywhere else the same sheet is drawn without it, and nothing is missing but the bend.
  • Two components of its own. A segmented control whose selection is a lit lens, and a rail of three tiles — paintings, languages, trackers — where every number is checked by the test suite against the release.
  • Built for a slow phone. No image on the default screen, AVIF at 640 pixels in the gallery, and a cheap mode that drops the blur pass entirely when the device asks for it.

07

Four steps,
no shortcuts.

The route from a question to a sentence you can point at, in the order it happens.

  1. 01

    Your pages, indexed on your device

    Add a chapter and it is split into passages and indexed in the browser. The books never leave the phone; nothing is uploaded to a server to be answered from.

  2. 02

    Retrieve before writing

    The question is matched against those passages first. A follow-up like “why is it important?” is read against the question before it, so the topic carries instead of resetting.

  3. 03

    Cite, or decline

    If the pages are thin, the answer says so and offers the labelled web search instead. Outside material is always marked as outside, never quietly mixed in.

  4. 04

    You check it by hand

    Every citation names the file and the page. Open the page, find the sentence. If it is not there, that is a bug. The judge page says where to report it.

about studying.
