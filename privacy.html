THE SMALL PRINT / 01

Privacy
policy.

What stays on your device, what may be sent elsewhere, and the choices you have.

Last updated 28 October 2026

01 / The short version

Vignan Vidyanikethan AI is a student-built study project for the INSPIRE-MANAK science fair. You can use it as a guest. Books and chats are stored in your browser. For new installations, the one-tap Search the web if needed switch is on: if your books do not match, a search query goes to Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo (plus a built-in school reference) unless you switch it off in the chat box or at the top of Settings. Public links you ask the app to read are sent through the site's server. Connected AIs and accounts also use outside services.

02 / On your device

The app stores imported books and extracted pages, search indexes, chats, decks, flashcard reviews, quiz results, settings and any keys you enter in browser storage. Preferences and streaks may also use local storage; a session-only flag remembers the entry choice. A service worker caches the app for offline use. If storage is blocked or full, some data may exist only in the current tab.

Model-paper previews are assembled from selected documents on the device and remain in the current tab; downloaded or printed copies are separate. Signing out does not erase device data. On a shared device, use Settings → Delete all local data and remove any downloaded files. That action does not delete your server account, shared copies, browser caches, or separately downloaded AI models; use the model controls or your browser's site-data settings for those.

03 / Accounts and leaderboard

To register, you provide a username, password, age, gender choice and, where applicable, a referral code and a declaration of guardian permission. The server receives the age and stores an age band rather than the exact age. Passwords are handled by the configured account backend, which stores password hashes rather than returning passwords to the app. Cookies support login and request security.

When signed in, quiz summaries such as topic, score, date and points are sent to the account service to update progress, streaks and the leaderboard. Full books, chats, decks and question-by-question reviews are not account-synced. The leaderboard can show your username and study statistics to other visitors; it does not show your age band or gender. Choose a nickname that does not reveal your identity.

The operator of this deployment — the school or the person running it — can open a private console that lists account records: username, age band, gender choice, signup date and progress totals. It requires a secret configured on the server, every visit is written to the server log, and it cannot show passwords, cannot sign in as a student, and cannot read books, chats, decks or answers, because those never leave your browser. It exists so a guardian can ask what is held, and so the school can keep a backup. The configured operator may use Supabase or a local server store for accounts. Infrastructure providers can receive ordinary request metadata, such as an IP address, for hosting, security or diagnostics. This app does not include an analytics tracker. Retention and deletion of server records are controlled by the project operator; the app does not currently offer self-service account deletion.

04 / AI, search and outside services

Bolt's cited-excerpt lookup runs in the browser. Optional WebLLM models run on your device after a first download from model hosts; that download contacts those hosts. Other configured local endpoints may be on your own device or network.

New installations enable the web-search switch. If your library has no matching passage, your search query, which may include a recent topic from the conversation, is sent through the site server to the Wikipedia edition for your interface language (33 languages are offered), to the DuckDuckGo Instant Answer API, and to the search provider you selected. The site server may then read the top results (up to two pages) so it can quote a real passage instead of a short snippet. You can turn it off with the labelled switch in the chat box or at the top of Settings; previous saved opt-outs stay off. Search queries and results may be cached temporarily in server memory for about five minutes. Outside excerpts are labelled separately and are not independently verified.

When you paste a public link in Ask or import one in Library, the URL is sent to the site server, which contacts the destination website even if web fallback is off. That website and the hosting provider receive ordinary request metadata. The extracted content may be stored in your browser or shown in chat; source URLs and chat excerpts may appear in local exports. Do not submit private links, access tokens, personal details or sensitive questions. Blocking known credential-bearing URLs cannot detect every secret.

If you use a connected AI, your question, instructions and relevant library excerpts may be sent to the chosen provider, directly or through this site's server. Providers have their own retention and training rules; check their terms first. Student API keys stay in browser storage and are sent with a request to the site's proxy when selected; keys for directly configured providers may be sent to those endpoints. They are excluded from the app's JSON export and account/leaderboard records, but anyone with access to that browser may be able to see or use them.

05 / Sharing and external links

Result videos, posters and captions are created on your device. The score and topic are visible if you choose to share them; nickname and invite code are optional. Copies you download or publish are outside the app's control. The optional Zero Vast link and QR code open an external website with its own privacy practices.

06 / Children and guardians

Students under 13 are asked to confirm a parent or guardian's permission when registering. This is self-attested and is not independently verified. A parent, guardian or school should supervise use and arrange any consent and safeguards required where the app is used. Guest use does not create an account, but local storage and optional external services still matter.

07 / Questions and changes

For questions, access or deletion of server-side account records, ask the school or person operating this deployment. No dedicated contact channel has been supplied in the project. The operator should add one and review this policy, retention arrangements and child-consent processes with appropriate legal guidance before public use. This page may change as the project changes; the date above identifies the current version.
