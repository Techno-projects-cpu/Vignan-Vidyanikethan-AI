Acceptable use
& academic integrity.

The short version is one sentence: this is a study assistant, and using it to hand in work you did not do is against this policy and against the school's rules.

Last updated 27 October 2026

01 / What this is for

Asking questions, working through a problem you are stuck on, checking your own reasoning, turning your notes into revision cards, practising questions, reading a public page you were given, and finding out what you do not yet understand. These uses are the reason the project exists and they are always allowed.

02 / What is not allowed

Submitting generated text as your own work where the school requires your own; sitting an assessment with the assistant open; asking for an answer to a live exam or a marked assignment; pasting another student's private work; using the site to harass, impersonate or bully anyone; attempting to break, overload or scrape the service; probing accounts that are not yours; and using the assistant to produce content that is illegal in India or that sexualises, threatens or degrades a minor.

03 / Academic integrity in practice

Cite the assistant the way your school asks you to cite a tutor: as help received, not as a source of truth. Write the final answer yourself, in your own words, and be able to explain every step without the app open. When a cited answer gives you a page, open that page — the citation is the part you can check. If you cannot explain a sentence you copied, you have not learned it yet, and a teacher will notice.

04 / Your account, your responsibility

Do not share your password or your session. Anything done through your account is treated as done by you. On a shared or school device, sign out and use Delete all local data when you finish, because chats, notes and exported files stay in that browser.

05 / Machine keys and costs

The project runs on free tiers that other students also use. Do not bulk-generate content, do not automate requests against the site, and do not use a shared school key outside studying. Rate limits exist for that reason, and bypassing them is a breach of this policy, not a clever trick.

06 / What happens if this is broken

The project operator may rate-limit, suspend or remove an account, and may report serious misuse — threats, harassment, attempts to break the service — to the school. Because the project is a student build, it cannot detect every case and does not monitor private chats; enforcement usually follows a report from a teacher or a student rather than automation. If you see misuse, tell the operator or a teacher.

07 / Honest limits

This policy is written by a student for a school project and is not legal advice. Where it conflicts with your school's code of conduct or the law, the school's rules and the law apply. If a teacher sets a stricter rule for their class — no AI at all during a topic, for example — that rule wins over anything written here.
